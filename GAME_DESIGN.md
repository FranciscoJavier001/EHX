# Game design

The operator starts in Surface with optional assignments, a virtual device registry, and the event stream. The four stories share a timing discrepancy. Correlate a relay with a fragmented signal to make a Shadow contact. Combine the cipher and dispatch to unlock an Abyss ledger. Show OMEGA the story title after reading the ledger to learn how the stories connect.

Defense creates a parallel operational loop. Open incidents have evidence in Forensics, SIGINT and Archive. Isolation suspends a virtual session. Restoration puts the node back into service and resolves open incidents tied to it. Pending link requests may be rejected. All these actions modify the same world.

Operator and autonomous modes show the live universe. Cinematic mode foregrounds the topology, live activity and automatic terminal, requests fullscreen where supported, and can be exited through Settings or with Escape. Quiet mode slows updates and suppresses sound and animation.

## BLACK

Security firms pay the operator in credits to review contradictory audit files. The energy and aviation reports expose a shared approval stamp and unlock a frontier commission. A separate cyber incident file exposes a double payment. The frontier dispatch leads to an aborted drone operation and a communiqué issued before the order. Publishing the assembled evidence forfeits the escrow. Sealing it releases the escrow and commits the operator to the cover story.
